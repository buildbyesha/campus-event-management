About the project

Purpose

This portal gives students one place to discover campus events and register, and gives organizers one place to manage seats, participants and updates.

Key modules

Event creation

Organizers add schedule, venue and eligibility.

Student registration

A validated form for every event.

Seat-limit management

Events close when capacity is reached.

Live seat count

Seats filled shown on every event.

Confirmation slips

Printable proof of registration.

Notifications and dashboard

Alerts and capacity tracking for organizers.

Objective and benefits

Objective: replace paper forms and scattered notices with one clear system.

  • Students: see availability first, register once, keep a slip.
  • Organizers: avoid over-registration and track participation easily.
